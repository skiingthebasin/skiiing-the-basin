Alta Toquima Traverse

Linking Mt. Jefferson's Summits

Part I · Approach

Leaving the Sagebrush Behind

Something interesting happens when a stingy winter rolls around. The baseline for chase-worthy storms drops considerably, and typically outrageous plans suddenly seem like a pretty good idea.

I had been eyeing an incoming mid-April refresh, hoping it would align with the weekend. Alas, the storm slowed and it became evident that Tuesday would be the day to score the best conditions. Leo was down to join, and Andrew gave the thumbs up as well, still riding the high of our successful tour in the Copper Mountains a couple weekends prior.

Leo and I departed Reno Monday night, the miles and hours buzzed by in a strange post-work disequilibrium. I had keyed in on an unskied project peak, Mt. Jefferson (high point of the Toquima Range), which had supposedly picked up a foot+ of fresh powder. Andrew had left Salt Lake City a day earlier and had sent promising photos, his truck parked in fluffy snow at the Love's gas station in Ely.

I was eager to see what lay in store for us, especially since OpenSnow wasn't predicting any snow on the ground in the Toquimas. The whole plan hinged on a single promising pre-storm satellite image. The die had been cast.
