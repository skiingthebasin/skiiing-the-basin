The Idea

To ski every peak in Nevada, USA with over 3,000‘ of prominence.

Why?

To lure myself into skiing Nevada’s lesser-visited places. Nevada offers several lifetimes of adventure skiing, get out and experience it for yourself!

What is Prominence?

Prominence is a method of quantifying a peak’s relief. It is the minimum vertical drop you would descend from a peak to climb to a higher summit. Due to it’s unique basin & range geology, Nevada has more highly prominent peaks than any other state in the Lower 48.
